AWD

All-Wheel Drive (AWD) provides enhanced traction and control, making it easier to drive in challenging conditions like snow, rain, or rough terrain.

×

Adaptive Cruise Control

Adaptive Cruise Control automatically adjusts your speed to maintain a safe distance from the vehicle ahead, ensuring a stress-free driving experience.

×

Android Auto

Android Auto allows you to connect your Android smartphone to the car's infotainment system, providing access to apps, navigation, and music.

×

Apple CarPlay

Apple CarPlay integrates your iPhone with the car's infotainment system, enabling hands-free calls, navigation, and access to your favorite apps.

×

Backup Camera

The Backup Camera provides a clear view of the area behind your vehicle, making parking and reversing safer and easier.

×

Blind Spot Monitor

The Blind Spot Monitor alerts you to vehicles in your blind spots, helping you change lanes safely.

×

Bluetooth

Bluetooth connectivity allows you to make hands-free calls and stream music wirelessly from your smartphone.

×

Interior Accents

Interior accents add a touch of style and sophistication to the cabin, enhancing the overall driving experience.

×

Keyless Entry

Keyless entry allows you to unlock and start your vehicle without removing the key fob from your pocket or bag.

×

Lane Departure Warning

Lane Departure Warning alerts you if you unintentionally drift out of your lane, helping you stay on course.

×

Parking Sensors / Assist

Parking sensors assist you in parking by detecting obstacles and providing audible alerts to help you avoid collisions.

×

Power Seats

Power seats allow you to easily adjust the seating position for optimal comfort and support.

×

Satellite Radio Ready

Satellite radio provides access to a wide range of music, news, and entertainment channels, ensuring you stay entertained on the road.

×

WiFi Hotspot

The WiFi hotspot feature allows you to connect your devices to the internet while on the go, keeping you connected wherever you are.
